# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A financially stable heavy BNPL user who runs Klarna, PayPal and Riverty at the same time.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To keep the control that made her choose BNPL by seeing everything she owes in one place.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** At month-end she opens three apps and copies amounts into a spreadsheet, because Riverty card spend takes up to 24 hours to appear, due dates sit on two screens, and some charges show a processor name instead of the merchant. A repayment notification arrived after the due date, so she paid a reminder fee. "I'm not broke, I just can't see everything in one place."
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** A self-built spreadsheet with three columns: what, how much, which date. Manual copying between the Klarna, PayPal and Riverty apps. Memory and matching by amount to identify charges.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Opens each app separately. Klarna, PayPal and Riverty each hold part of what she owes. Riverty alone splits card purchases and checkout purchases across two screens, so that is four views, not three.
2. Copies each purchase into the spreadsheet, recording item, amount and due date by hand.
3. Tries to recognise unclear lines. Where Riverty shows a processor name instead of the merchant, she has to work out what the charge was.
4. Allows for lag. Card spend can take up to 24 hours to appear, so a recent purchase may be missing when she reconciles.
5. Relies on her own dates rather than the app's reminders. A Riverty notification arrived after the due date, so the spreadsheet becomes her real source of truth.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Copies each purchase into the spreadsheet, recording item, amount and due date by hand
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** I'm not broke, I just can't see everything in one place.
