# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Riverty card |
| Goal | Awareness |
| Launch tier | L, Multi-channel |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Awareness, As this entire product (not a product feature) is being launched at the first time ever, awareness is the key. If the market does not know we offer the product, they can not start engaging or converting.
- **Target audience:** a financially stable heavy BNPL user who runs Klarna, PayPal and Riverty at the same time

## Launch Tier
- **L, Multi-channel**, We need coverage from multi-channel approach as we are launching a completely new product. Not having the coverage risks that nobody will know about the product and there will be no adoption/conversion/revenue

## Channels
1. **Paid: Paid campaign in collaboration with a selected key merchants in the target market & location via YouTube ads**
2. **Earned: A product promo in key merchant shops**
3. **Owned: A major in-app promo for the Riverty card - a popup screen that every user in the target location will see.**

## Enablement & Assets
in-app onboarding flow, collaboration campaign assets

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns the plan. Marketing team builds the needed marketing assets. Product marketing will drive the paid campaign together with marketing team. Key accounts & sales will initiate the key merchant discussions and drive the exposure. In-app experience is built by the app team using the marketing assets created by marketing.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Key merchant discussions, marketing material production begins. Week 2: in-app experience build begins, purchasing and timing the paid channel week 3: key merchants lock in, marketing materials ready week 4: in app ready week 5: launch day, paid channels live week 6 monitor, collect feedback

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** new user signups, time to first card transactions, number of conversions
- **Bad signal to watch for:** high reach, zero signups
- **Likely post-launch decision:** double-down
