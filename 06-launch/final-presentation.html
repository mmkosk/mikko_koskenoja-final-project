Slide 5 · Strategy

Problem hook & value proposition

Problem hook

Riverty's users like Lena juggle several BNPL apps and pay at the till with someone else's card, while Klarna and PayPal grow into the stores and checkouts Riverty can't reach, and with a brand preference of only 6%, every month without a card moves Riverty closer to being a replaceable checkout button.

Data-backed hypothesis

Based on the 15 qualitative interviews (the overview was rated a USP and equated with a budget book, and losing track of due dates was the most frequent concern) and the Norstat survey of 1,022 users, I believe that giving Lena, a mobile-first heavy BNPL user, one real-time view of her card spend, merchant names and due dates will turn the Riverty card from an occasional checkout option into the place where she checks and settles her spending, as measured by [X]% of active card users opening the overview at least weekly. I will protect existing-BNPL volume per heavy user against a matched control and will make a go/no-go decision after two billing cycles in the friends-and-family pilot and three billing cycles in an online-only external cohort (about 5-6 months in total).

Value proposition

For busy BNPL users like Lena, who want money to leave their account only after they've decided to keep what they bought, the Riverty card lets them pay later anywhere, in stores and online, with one clear view of what's due. Unlike Klarna's debit-first card or PayPal's separate instalment products, it keeps the "decide first, pay after" habit in every setting and sends users back to Riverty's partner merchants instead of away from them.

New value

Riverty moves from a checkout option Lena doesn't prefer to an everyday card she carries. That closes the in-store and heavy-user gaps from the brief and gives Riverty a direct consumer relationship for the first time.

Why now
  • Klarna's card base is growing, and PayPal now offers instalments at Amazon.de and a virtual card for in-store payments, so Lena's habits are being set by competitors.
  • From 20 November 2026, most BNPL falls under consumer credit rules, and Riverty carries that compliance cost with or without a card.
  • Riverty's banking licence and Mastercard platform are already in place, and a consumer card is the next product that can earn that investment.

The merchant benefit is a hypothesis, not something the brief establishes, so it should be one of the first things tested in discovery.

Slide 6 · Research

Competitive alternatives & the workaround

Who & goal

Role: A financially stable heavy BNPL user who runs Klarna, PayPal and Riverty at the same time.

Goal: To keep the control that made her choose BNPL by seeing everything she owes in one place.

What she uses today
KlarnaPayPalRivertySelf-built spreadsheet

A self-built spreadsheet with three columns: what, how much, which date. Manual copying between the Klarna, PayPal and Riverty apps. Memory and matching by amount to identify charges.

Core frustration

Copies each purchase into the spreadsheet, recording item, amount and due date by hand

Evidence

"I'm not broke, I just can't see everything in one place."

Open journey map file

Journey map · the process, step by step
STEP 1Opens each app separately.Klarna, PayPal and Riverty each hold part of what she owes. Riverty alone splits card purchases and checkout purchases across two screens, so that is four views, not three.
Moment of miserySTEP 2Copies each purchase into the spreadsheet,recording item, amount and due date by hand.
STEP 3Tries to recognise unclear lines.Where Riverty shows a processor name instead of the merchant, she has to work out what the charge was.
STEP 4Allows for lag.Card spend can take up to 24 hours to appear, so a recent purchase may be missing when she reconciles.
STEP 5Relies on her own dates rather than the app's reminders.A Riverty notification arrived after the due date, so the spreadsheet becomes her real source of truth.

Must-haves
  • M1 Card overview screen: list of every card transaction (amount, date, merchant, status) with a month total
  • M2 Near-real-time feed: authorisations appear as "pending" and move to "settled"
  • M4 One due-date list: card invoices and Riverty Pay invoices in a single list sorted by due date, each with amount owed. Read-only, reusing existing invoice data

M3 Merchant name resolution: show the merchant, not the processor descriptor. Fall back to the raw descriptor, labelled as such. It is not preventing her to see the overview.

Slide 9 · Launch

GTM strategy & success metrics

Goal

Awareness. As this entire product (not a product feature) is being launched at the first time ever, awareness is the key. If the market does not know we offer the product, they can not start engaging or converting.

Audience

a financially stable heavy BNPL user who runs Klarna, PayPal and Riverty at the same time
